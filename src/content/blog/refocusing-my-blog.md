The last thing I posted here was a note about how to add an ellipsis to a line of text with CSS. That was eleven years ago. It was a fine post, and the trick still works, but it isn't what I want this place to be anymore.

Most of what I've learned since then hasn't been about code. It's been about the parts of the job nobody hands you a tutorial for: Deciding which fire to let burn. Balancing technical long term planning with short term business needs. Telling someone their work isn't at the level it needs to be, and doing it in a way they can actually use. Noticing that the technically correct answer and the right answer for the team are sometimes different things, and that every team is different in what they choose to prioritize.

I led engineering teams in healthtech at Oscar and Thirty Madison, and I lead them at Tesla now. While I continue to be technically dangerous in my craft, I've found myself gravitating towards building relationships, building up engineers, and building out product direction.

So I'm changing what this blog is about. What I expect to write here:

- How the role of leaders and engineers have changed and will continue to change 
- The career decisions that feel small at the time and turn out not to be
- Technical achievements and the non-technical approaches to solving those problems 
- The things I got wrong, which is most of the interesting material

I don't have a cadence in mind. I'd rather post something I've genuinely thought about every few weeks than something on time.
